About

Welcome to my academic portfolio. I am Nahin — a Computer Vision researcher and educator holding a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). Currently, I serve as a Lecturer at Bangladesh Army University of Science & Technology, Khulna (BAUSTK), where I share my passion for cutting-edge technologies with the next generation of engineers.

My primary research is centered on Computer Vision and Machine Learning, with multiple published works spanning deep learning-based license plate recognition, vehicle classification, image colorization, and environmental quality prediction. I thrive on bridging theoretical foundations with real-world applications.

Beyond research, I bring leadership experience from my tenure as President of the Jashore Association of KUET (JAK), where I honed abilities in team management and strategic planning. I approach every challenge with technical rigor, creative thinking, and a commitment to continuous growth.

I invite you to explore my work and connect to discuss potential collaborations in computer vision or academic initiatives.

Gray Scale to Color Image Conversion using Generative Adversarial Networks
Under review in a Q1 journal  ·  2025

This study explores image colorization using deep learning, particularly conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (cGANs), which outperform traditional reference-based methods by generating realistic colors more efficiently and without requiring manual reference images.

02
Journal Article
Comparative Analysis of Machine Learning Models for Predicting Water Quality Index in Dhaka's Rivers of Bangladesh
Environmental Sciences Europe  ·  Springer Nature  ·  2025
Impact Factor: 6.0 (2023)  |  5-Year IF: 6.6

This study addresses severe pollution in Dhaka's key rivers due to industrialization and inadequate waste management. Fourteen machine learning models were evaluated for predicting the Water Quality Index (WQI). Artificial Neural Network (ANN) and Random Forest Regressor demonstrated the highest accuracy, with ANN achieving top overall performance metrics — offering a reliable, cost-effective method for environmental monitoring and policy decision-making.

03
Conference Paper Best Paper Award
Automatic License Plate Recognition System for Bangladeshi Vehicles Using Deep Neural Network
International Conference on Big Data, IoT and Machine Learning (BIM 2021)  ·  Springer Singapore  ·  2022

This paper proposes an optimal end-to-end ALPR system for Bangladeshi vehicles by experimenting with various DNN models trained on a rich, novel dataset of Bangladeshi vehicles and license plates. A custom algorithm eliminates the typical segmentation phase, yielding 99.37% accuracy in plate localization and 96.31% accuracy in text recognition. Awarded Best Paper in the Machine Learning track.

04
Conference Paper
Bangladeshi Local Vehicle Recognition with a Comprehensive Dataset using Transfer Learning Techniques
IEEE  ·  2023

A challenging dataset of Bangladeshi vehicles was developed to assess model performance on Intelligent Transport System (ITS) tasks. Various pre-trained models were trained and evaluated; the proposed model achieved leading results in both localization and categorization. The dataset was shown to better reflect real-life scenarios than pre-existing alternatives.

Automatic License Plate Recognizer

Seven DNN architectures were benchmarked including EfficientDet variants, SSD MobileNet, and SSD ResNet50, before arriving at the final optimised configuration. Developed as an undergraduate capstone project.
