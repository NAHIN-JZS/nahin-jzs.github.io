About

Welcome to my academic portfolio. I am Nahin — a Computer Vision researcher and educator holding a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET). Currently, I serve as a Lecturer at Bangladesh Army University of Science & Technology, Khulna (BAUSTK), where I share my passion for cutting-edge technologies with the next generation of engineers.

My primary research is centred on Computer Vision and Machine Learning, with multiple published works spanning deep learning-based license plate recognition, vehicle classification, image colorization, and environmental quality prediction. I thrive on bridging theoretical foundations with real-world applications.

Beyond research, I bring leadership experience from my tenure as President of the Jashore Association of KUET (JAK), and I find creative expression through photography — a discipline that sharpens the same attention to detail I bring to my academic work.

I invite you to explore my work and connect to discuss potential collaborations in computer vision or academic initiatives.

Gray Scale to Color Image Conversion using Generative Adversarial Networks
Under review in a Q1 journal  ·  2025

This study explores image colorization using deep learning, particularly conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (cGANs), which outperform traditional reference-based methods by generating realistic colors more efficiently and without requiring manual reference images.

02
Journal Article
Comparative Analysis of Machine Learning Models for Predicting Water Quality Index in Dhaka's Rivers of Bangladesh
Environmental Sciences Europe  ·  Springer Nature  ·  2025  ·  IF: 6.0 (2023)

Fourteen machine learning models were evaluated for predicting the Water Quality Index (WQI) in Dhaka's key rivers. ANN and Random Forest Regressor demonstrated the highest accuracy — offering a reliable, cost-effective method for environmental monitoring and policy decision-making.

03
Conference Paper Best Paper Award
Automatic License Plate Recognition System for Bangladeshi Vehicles Using Deep Neural Network
BIM 2021  ·  Springer Singapore  ·  2022

An optimal end-to-end ALPR system for Bangladeshi vehicles trained on a novel dataset. A custom algorithm eliminates the segmentation phase, yielding 99.37% accuracy in plate localization and 96.31% in text recognition. Awarded Best Paper in the Machine Learning track.

Read the Paper
04
Conference Paper
Bangladeshi Local Vehicle Recognition with a Comprehensive Dataset using Transfer Learning Techniques
IEEE  ·  2023

A challenging dataset of Bangladeshi vehicles was developed for ITS tasks. Various pre-trained models were evaluated; the proposed model achieved leading results in both localization and categorization, better reflecting real-life scenarios than pre-existing alternatives.
